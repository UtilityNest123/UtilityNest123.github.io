Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At UtilityNest (accessible at https://utilitynest123.github.io/) and our connected sub-platforms—WebToolBox, Calcvora, ZeptoSolve, and DD Bazar—protecting visitor privacy is a foundational priority. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and how it is handled.

1. Client-Side Processing & User Files

UtilityNest is built on a privacy-first, client-side architecture. When you use our image resizers, PDF converters, currency calculators, or engineering simulators, all file processing and mathematical computations occur locally within your own web browser. We do not upload, view, store, or share your personal files or calculation inputs on any backend server.

  • UtilityNest has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or reviewing the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

UtilityNest's Privacy Policy does not apply to external advertisers or third-party websites linked from our pages. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings at any time.

5. Children's Information

UtilityNest does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII) from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our contact channels, please notify us immediately so we can remove it.
